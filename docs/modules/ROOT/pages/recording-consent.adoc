Version 1.0

This is the agreement between you, a volunteer who records your voice, and Zirek ("we", the maintainers of the https://github.com/ZirekHQ[ZirekHQ] projects).
It is written in plain words on purpose.
If anything is unclear, ask before you record.
Recording is always your choice and you can stop at any time.

== In short

* Your recordings are shared publicly for *non-commercial* use, with credit to the project (and to you, if you want).
* A *business* can use your voice only by making a written agreement with Zirek. We publish every such agreement.
* Money from those agreements pays for the project: [recording, checking, hosting and training costs. Ali to confirm].
* A voice that has been published cannot be taken back. You can have your recordings removed before training starts.

== What you give us

"Recordings" means the audio files you make for the project, the text you read, and the models trained on them.

== 1. Public licence (non-commercial)

You license your Recordings to everyone under https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0] (CC BY-NC 4.0).
Anyone may use, copy, train on and share them for non-commercial purposes if they give credit to the Zirek project.
Free apps, screen readers, schools, charities and research are all non-commercial uses.

== 2. Commercial use only by agreement

You also give Zirek the right to license your Recordings, and models trained on them, to businesses for commercial use.
This right is non-exclusive: you keep the copyright and can license your own recordings elsewhere.
We will only do this under a written agreement that:

* names the business and what it may do,
* forbids using the voice to imitate you or anyone else without permission,
* is added to the public list of agreements in the {repo}[voice-models repository].

[Ali to decide: are volunteers allowed to say "non-commercial only, no business licence"? Simplest is one rule for everyone, because trained models mix many speakers. If you allow opt-outs, that speaker's voice is left out of any commercially licensed model.]

== 3. Where the money goes

[Ali to confirm before use.] Money Zirek receives under section 2 is used for the project: paying for recording and checking work, hosting and training.
We publish a short yearly summary of income and spending.

== 4. Published voices

Models trained on your Recordings may be published and used by anyone allowed under sections 1 and 2, including other projects.
A published model cannot be recalled.

== 5. Withdrawing

You can ask us to remove your Recordings from the dataset at any time before they go into a training run.
Once a model is released, your voice stays in it.

== 6. Your data

A voice identifies you.
We keep your audio, your name or pseudonym, your language and the date you agreed.
Only the maintainers can see them.
Audio stays in the shared Drive folder until it goes into a training run.
To have your data deleted, open an {repo}/issues[issue] that names only the pseudonym or credit you gave, never your real name or contact details.
[Add the controller's contact and retention period once a contact address exists, see voice-models#5.]

== 7. Credit

You choose whether the voice credits you by name, by pseudonym, or not at all.
Say which in your opening issue.
Credit to the project is always required under CC BY-NC.

== 8. Misuse

Anyone can copy a public voice, and we cannot stop a determined person from misusing it, for example to imitate you.
Our licences and agreements forbid it and we will act on reports, but we cannot promise it will never happen.
If you are not comfortable with that risk, do not record.

== 9. Your promises

* You are at least 18 years old, or a parent or guardian has agreed in writing.
* The voice is yours, and you read only the text we give you.
* You are not under any contract that stops you from agreeing to this.

== 10. Changes

If we change this agreement we give the new version a new number.
A new version applies only to recordings made after you agree to it.

== How to agree

In your opening issue write the line:

----
I agree to the recording consent v1.0
----

We keep a link to that issue as the record of your agreement.
